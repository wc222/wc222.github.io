About

Chao Wang is a Staff Engineer on the LongCat team at Meituan (Beidou Talent Program). The work focuses on LLM inference, including reliability, performance, and cost efficiency. Chao Wang received a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2026, advised by Prof. Ming-Chang Yang. Chao Wang’s publications include papers at OSDI, ICML, SC, and VLDB.
